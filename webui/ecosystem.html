Friends in the Space

We're glad this space is crowded — it means the problem matters. Giving AI agents durable, useful memory is one of the hard, important problems of this generation, and a lot of talented teams are building toward it from different angles. The tools below are colleagues we genuinely admire — each has shaped how the whole field thinks about memory. We're listing them here not to compare, but to celebrate.

And FaultLine?

We simply do things a bit differently. FaultLine is write-validated, walk-read memory — every fact is grounded and traceable at write time, then recalled by a deterministic walk of a per-tenant knowledge graph in PostgreSQL rather than fuzzy vector similarity. Same destination many of us are walking toward; a different path there.
